# Proposal — let a customer cancel an order

## Why now

Support handles cancellations by hand. Someone orders the wrong quantity, emails us within a few minutes, and an agent goes and edits the record. It is the third most common ticket type and none of it is tracked anywhere a customer can see.

Giving customers a cancel action removes the ticket and gives us a record of what was cancelled and when.

## What changes for the user

After placing an order, a customer can cancel it. Their order history then shows the order as cancelled rather than hiding it, so they can see what happened to it.

## Out of scope

Refunds. We do not take payment in this system yet, so there is nothing to return. When payment arrives, cancellation will need to trigger a refund and that will be its own change.
